TEXT OVERLAY (IMPORTANT)
* Add bold text in the upper-left corner
* Example: BUY THIS?”
* Text should be large, clean, and highly readable
* White or bright text with dark outline/shadow for contrast
* Do NOT let text overlap the product or face
* Background behind text should be simple enough for readability
LAYOUT
* Product on the left (largest, closest to camera)
* Person on the right (smaller, slightly behind)
* Product should NOT be centered
* Camera slightly pulled back with breathing room
PRODUCT (MAIN FOCUS)
* Biggest and sharpest object in frame
* Sitting naturally on a desk
* Realistic materials (glass, metal, reflections)
* No distortion or redesign
* Immediately grabs attention
PERSON (SUPPORTING)
* Smaller than product
* Natural reaction (curious, skeptical, impressed)
* Looking at or interacting with the product
* No exaggerated YouTube face
BACKGROUND
* Real desk/workspace
* Slight blur (depth of field)
* Darker and less saturated than product
* No clutter, no fake lighting
STYLE / CAMERA
* Real photo look (not AI art)
* 35–50mm lens feel
* Soft indoor lighting
* Natural shadows and skin texture
* No glow or heavy effects
PRIORITY
1. Product
2. Human reaction
3. Text supports both
GOAL
* High CTR, realistic, trustworthy
* Product is the hero
