PRODUCT FIDELITY (VERY IMPORTANT)
* The product must look like a real photographed commercial product
* It must be exactly consistent with a real-world item (no redesign, no reinterpretation)
* Preserve:
    * correct proportions
    * real materials (glass, metal, plastic, rubber, fabric)
    * accurate seams, buttons, ports, labels, and logos (if visible)
* If unsure, default to a generic but realistic product design (Apple / Samsung / Sony-level realism)
* Do NOT stylize or “improve” the product design in any way
* Do NOT make it look like a render, concept art, or 3D model
PRODUCT (MAIN FOCUS — STRICT REALISM)
* Closest + sharpest object in frame
* Held naturally in one hand (real grip, relaxed fingers)
* Must look like it was photographed in a real studio or real room
* No artificial smoothing, no plastic look, no waxy surfaces
* Visible imperfections allowed (micro-scratches, fingerprints, slight reflections)
* Natural lighting interaction (real specular highlights only)
* NO glow, NO bloom, NO CGI lighting
NEGATIVE PRODUCT RULES (IMPORTANT)
* no AI-generated object design
* no melted shapes or warped geometry
* no overly perfect “toy-like” surfaces
* no exaggerated thickness or unrealistic curves
* no fake branding or random symbols
* no fantasy redesign of the device

TEXT (TOP-LEFT)
* Bold: BUY THIS?
* Upper-left corner only
* High contrast (white with dark outline/shadow)
* Must NOT overlap product or face

LAYOUT
* Product on LEFT (foreground, closest to camera, ~15–20% width)
* Person on RIGHT (background, slightly blurred)
* Strong depth separation (product sharp → person soft)
* Camera pulled back, natural framing

PERSON (SECONDARY)
* Slightly out of focus (real shallow DOF blur)
* Pointing at product naturally
* Expression: curious / skeptical / impressed
* Real skin texture, no exaggeration

BACKGROUND
* Real desk / workspace environment
* Slight blur
* Darker + less saturated than product
* Clean and believable (no clutter overload)

CAMERA / STYLE
* Photorealistic DSLR look (NOT CGI)
* 50mm lens, f/1.8–f/2.8 depth of field
* Natural indoor lighting (window or soft lamp)
* Real shadows, real reflections
* Slight color boost only (no HDR, no glow, no bloom)

VISUAL PRIORITY
1. Product (must feel real and tangible)
2. Human reaction
3. Text overlay
